## What is flexbio.link?

**flexbio.link** is a link-in-bio platform made for people who want more than a basic list of links.

Create a public profile, make it look exactly how you want, connect your socials, show your Discord presence, add music and widgets, customize animations and effects, and share everything through one clean URL.

> `flexbio.link/yourname`

Core profile creation and personalization are free. Optional purchases are reserved for extras such as badges and marketplace items.

## Built to be shared

Your profile can be reached through a simple username URL:

```text
https://flexbio.link/yourname
```

Depending on your setup, the same page can also be shared through an alias or your own flexbio.link subdomain.

Customize how the page appears when pasted into Discord or social platforms with your own embed title, description, image and color.

## Getting started

1. Go to **[flexbio.link](https://flexbio.link)**.
2. Claim the username you want.
3. Create your account.
4. Open the dashboard and customize your profile.
5. Add links, widgets, music and effects.
6. Share your page anywhere.
