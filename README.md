## One link. All of you.

Your profile should not look like everyone else's.

Start with a username, avatar, bio and links. Then turn it into something personal with layouts, fonts, effects, media, widgets and polished sharing.

- <img src="./assets/icons/paintbrush.svg" alt="Customization" width="15" /> **Deep customization** — colors, gradients, fonts, cards, banners, backgrounds and profile styling.
- <img src="./assets/icons/sparkles.svg" alt="Effects" width="15" /> **Motion & effects** — entrance screens, animated borders, cursor effects, click effects and more.
- <img src="./assets/icons/music-4.svg" alt="Music" width="15" /> **Built for music** — upload tracks, add lyrics, choose player styles and enable audio visuals.
- <img src="./assets/icons/blocks.svg" alt="Widgets" width="15" /> **Live widgets** — Discord, Spotify, GitHub, Twitch, TikTok, clocks, weather, galleries and more.
- <img src="./assets/icons/bar-chart-3.svg" alt="Analytics" width="15" /> **Useful tools** — analytics, templates, image hosting, playground and multiple profile versions.

## What you get

- <img src="./assets/icons/users.svg" alt="Profiles" width="15" /> **Up to 5 independent profile versions** in one account.
- <img src="./assets/icons/link-2.svg" alt="Sharing" width="15" /> **Clean share links** with `flexbio.link/yourname` and `yourname.flexbio.link`.
- <img src="./assets/icons/badge-check.svg" alt="No branding" width="15" /> **No forced branding or watermark** on your profile.
- <img src="./assets/icons/layout-template.svg" alt="Layouts" width="15" /> **Flexible layouts** from simple bio pages to full portfolio layouts.
- <img src="./assets/icons/image.svg" alt="Assets" width="15" /> **Your own assets** — fonts, images, videos, music and custom icons.
- <img src="./assets/icons/share-2.svg" alt="Previews" width="15" /> **Better sharing** with custom previews, aliases and QR-friendly links.

Optional shop items, badges and marketplace extras stay optional — the core builder and customization remain free.

## Get started

1. Go to **[flexbio.link](https://flexbio.link)** and claim your username.
2. Add your bio, socials and links.
3. Pick a layout and style it your way.
4. Drop in music, widgets and effects if you want.
5. Share your page — changes go live instantly.

```text
flexbio.link/yourname
yourname.flexbio.link
```
